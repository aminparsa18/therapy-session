# Unsent letter

This letter is **not sent**. Its job is to get what is inside, outside. Write for 15-20 minutes without editing. Stop whenever you need to; you can skip any part.

**To:** (a person, your younger self, a part of you, or a loss)

Suggested flow, in your own words:
- What happened, or what I wish had happened:
- What I felt then, and what I feel now:
- What I needed and didn't get:
- What I want them to understand:
- What I'm ready to let go of, or keep:
- What I want to say to myself now:

## After writing
- How do I feel (0-10, one word)?
- What surprised me?
- What do I want to do with this letter: keep, tear up, read again next week?
