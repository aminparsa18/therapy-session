## In the window
For each worry:
1. Is it something I can act on? **Yes:** write one next step and when. **No:** it's a feeling or an uncertainty to carry; say "I can't solve this today."
2. What's the exact question underneath it?
3. What do I know for sure, and what am I afraid of?

Stop when the timer ends, even mid-thought.

## End of week
- Which worries came true? How did I cope?
- What did the window change?
